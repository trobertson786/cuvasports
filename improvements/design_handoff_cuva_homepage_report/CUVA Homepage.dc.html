OPTION A - PLAYFAIR DISPLAY (CURRENT)

Spain 1-0 Argentina AET: Ferran Torres Wins the World Cup for Spain

Set at 17px, the thin strokes start to disappear on the newsprint background.

High contrast, more display-like. Holds at 60px; fragile in card headlines and standfirsts.

OPTION B - SOURCE SERIF 4 (RECOMMENDED)

Spain 1-0 Argentina AET: Ferran Torres Wins the World Cup for Spain

At 17px the same face is still solid, so one serif can carry headlines and body.

Lower contrast, optical sizing, wider weight range. Survives 15px card headlines, which Playfair does not.
