3 · BREAKPOINT SPEC TO IMPLEMENT AGAINST

Breakpoints

Range Tailwind What changes
320-389base16px gutters. Everything else as 390. Nav labels hold at 14px.
390-429baseDesign target. 20px gutters, h1 32px, body 18px, single column throughout.
430-639min-[430px]h1 34px. Nothing else moves; the extra 40px goes to the measure.
640-767smGutters return to 24px. Cricket thumbnail cards may pair two up. Results rows stay stacked.

1024-1179lgDesktop nav bar, 12 column homepage grid, 32px gutters. Report page still single column, no rail.

Type on mobile

  • Report h1 Playfair 700 at 32px from 320, 34px from 430, 44px from 1024. Never below 20px anywhere.
  • Homepage lead h1 the same steps. Card headlines Source Serif 4 at 17-24px.
  • Body Source Serif 4 18px, line-height 1.6, which measures about 38 characters at 390 with 20px gutters and 42 at 430.
  • Metadata Inter 13px minimum, mono dates 12px, kickers 11px with 0.12em tracking, which is the one exception and it is uppercase and short.
  • Form inputs 16px so iOS does not zoom on focus.

Targets and spacing

  • 44px minimum on the smallest dimension for every target except links inside a sentence.
  • Utility strip, tags and footer keep 24px minimum between adjacent targets. Breadcrumb links are separated by a 14px gap either side of the divider, so the clear distance between two targets is 28px.
  • Footer links are 48px full-width rows with a hairline between, so the gap is unambiguous.
  • One call to action per card, and the whole card is the target.

iOS Safari and layout stability

  • svh for any full-height treatment, never dvh, never vh.
  • Footer pads to calc(28px + env(safe-area-inset-bottom)), so the real 28px still holds when the inset returns 0.
  • Every image slot declares aspect-ratio: 16/9 for lead and feature, 3/2 for hero and picture desk, 4/3 for thumbnails, 3/4 for the portrait. Nothing shifts as pictures load.
  • The lead photograph is the LCP element: it is the only eager image on the homepage, everything below it is lazy.
  • No carousels, no scroll-driven animation, no polling. The only interactive JS on mobile is the menu, the search toggle and the newsletter validation.

Kept from desktop, unchanged

  • 0 radii, 1px rules, 2px and 3px section rules, no shadows, no card fills. Any surviving rounded corner or white card on mobile is a bug.
  • Gold only on navy. Section colours and gold-ink carry labels on light surfaces.
  • Scores are aria-hidden blocks paired with a hidden sentence.
  • Hyphens only, British English, no invented data.

STILL OUTSTANDING, NOT SOLVED BY LAYOUT

  • Photography. The twelve reports without pictures now degrade honestly, but the Football section will keep looking thinner than Cricket until there are real photographs. This is the strongest case for the picture work.
  • Logos are still screenshot crops. The compact lockup is used at 38px on mobile, where a bitmap softens most. cuva-mark.svg and cuva-mark-white.svg are needed before launch.
  • The MDX source still records eleven goalkeeper saves against ten in the prose and in both designs.
