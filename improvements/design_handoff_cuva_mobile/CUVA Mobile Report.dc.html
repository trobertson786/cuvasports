Spain 1-0 Argentina AET: Ferran Torres Wins the World Cup for Spain

Ferran Torres came off the bench to score the only goal in extra time as Spain overcame ten-man Argentina 1-0 to lift the World Cup for the second time, despite a record ten-save display from Emiliano Martinez at MetLife Stadium.

CORRECTION · 20 JULY 2026, 09:14 BST

An earlier version of this report gave Emiliano Martinez eleven saves. He made ten. The figure has been corrected in the statistics table and in the standfirst.

SPAIN are champions of the world for the second time after Ferran Torres struck in extra time to defeat ten-man Argentina 1-0 in a tense and dramatic final at MetLife Stadium, ending the reign of Lionel Messi and his side as world champions.

Spain dominated the contest from the first whistle, keeping the ball with their trademark patience and pressing Argentina back for long periods. Argentina, so often dangerous on the counter, did not register a shot before the interval and went in level only because of their goalkeeper.

Martinez began his heroics in the twenty-second minute with the first of a series of important saves, and by the time Lamine Yamal struck a 25-yard free-kick in the seventy-fifth minute the Argentina goalkeeper was the reason the final was still goalless. That save, low and to his right, was the best of the ten he made.

The balance of the night changed three minutes into stoppage time at the end of normal time, when Enzo Fernandez was sent off for a second bookable offence. Argentina went to extra time with ten men and 0-0 on the board.

The goal came in the 106th minute. Nico Williams headed the ball back across the six-yard box and Torres, on as a substitute, finished from close range. A second Torres goal was ruled out four minutes later for an offside in the build-up, and Spain settled for one.

Argentina, down to ten men and finally beaten, could not respond, and Spain saw out the closing stages to be crowned champions of the world once more. Torres became just the second substitute to score the winning goal in a World Cup final, and Spain, so impressive throughout the tournament, were worthy winners.

For Messi and Argentina, the defence of their crown ended in heartbreak, but Emiliano Martinez left the field with his reputation only enhanced after a truly remarkable individual performance.
