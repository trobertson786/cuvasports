# CUVA Sports — Syndication & Licensing Outreach

Research and outreach drafts for getting CUVA Sports match reports distributed through third-party sports apps/platforms for a fee. Compiled 2026-08-07.

**Ground rule for all outreach:** don't claim partnerships, staff, or terms that don't exist yet. Every email below asks or offers a sample — none assumes a deal is already in motion.

## USA audience note — Wrexham angle

CUVA Sports will track **Wrexham AFC games occasionally**. For US-facing pitches (Yardbarker, World Soccer Talk, MSN when it reopens), this is worth surfacing explicitly — Wrexham has an outsized US following off the back of the Disney+ documentary and Reynolds/McElhenney ownership, and US soccer audiences actively search for Wrexham coverage that mainstream US outlets under-serve. It's a concrete, differentiated hook rather than "we cover the EFL generally" — worth a specific line in US pitches once there's actual published Wrexham coverage to point to.

## Outreach emails (psychology-optimized drafts)

Principles applied: lead with a low-friction offer (sample reports) instead of an open-ended question, specific subject lines over generic "enquiry", short and scannable, personal signature over corporate title, cut anything that asks the recipient to do work before they've seen the product.

### 1. Yardbarker
**Send via:** yardbarker.com contact form
**Subject:** English Premier League coverage — free sample for your network

> Hi there,
>
> I noticed Yardbarker's network carries syndicated coverage across MSN and FOX Sports — sending this in case there's a gap for English football specifically.
>
> I'm Theo, and I work with William Powell (FWA Life Member, covering English football since 1987) on CUVA Sports, a UK outlet running match reports across the Premier League, Championship and EFL — including occasional Wrexham coverage, which tends to draw strong US interest.
>
> Rather than ask you to evaluate us cold, I'd rather just send three recent reports so you can judge the quality yourself — no commitment either way. Worth a look?
>
> Theo Robertson
> cuvasports.com
