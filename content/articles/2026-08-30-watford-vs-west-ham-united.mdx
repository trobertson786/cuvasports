## The Story of the Match

Watford and West Ham United drew 1-1 at Vicarage Road in front of 20,976, the biggest crowd at the ground since Liverpool visited in the Premier League. Watford started the day eighth, West Ham 16th and still without a Championship win since relegation.

Watford came in off a Carabao Cup first round win over Crawley Town, a league win over Southampton, a 1-1 draw at Wrexham and a heavy midweek cup exit at home to Peterborough United. West Ham arrived after a cup win over Portsmouth, a 2-2 draw at Burnley, a derby defeat to Charlton Athletic and a 4-1 second round cup win at Southampton.

Alessio Dionisi named an unchanged side from the draw at Wrexham, with Imran Louza having joined Panathinaikos. Nuno Espirito Santo made three changes, Joel Veltman, Divine Mukasa and the debutant Airidas Golambeckis coming in for Konstantinos Mavropanos, Ollie Scarles and Joel Piroe, with James Ward-Prowse sold to Southampton in midweek.

West Ham started brightly in heavy rain but Watford took the lead in the 19th minute with a moment of quality, Nabizada dinking a right-footed chip over the goalkeeper and two defenders from inside the area after good work from captain Mattie Pollock. Iker Bravo almost added a second moments later, denied by a block and a Mads Hermansen save.

The equaliser arrived in the 27th minute. Kevin Keben was caught in possession inside his own area under pressure and Taty Castellanos' effort went in off Pollock for an own goal. Veltman was booked for delaying a restart, and Golambeckis headed over from a corner as West Ham finished the half on top.

Keben was booked early in the second half for a strong challenge on Mukasa. West Ham grew into the game through Bowen and Manor Solomon, Arne Engels curling narrowly wide from 20 yards. Dionisi turned to Martin Payero and Mamadou Doumbia on the hour, and Nuno sent on Piroe and Lewis Orford.

Watford tired but began to counter with more purpose in the closing quarter. Bravo forced a corner off Maximilian Kilman and Pollock produced a crucial challenge to deny Solomon inside the box in the 88th minute. Neither side could find a winner and the points were shared.

West Ham United host Wolverhampton Wanderers on Tuesday, while Watford travel to Swansea City the same night.
