## The Story of the Match

Queens Park Rangers rose to joint top of the SkyBet Championship with Swansea City and Charlton Athletic, moving onto 10 points from 4 games after beating Cardiff City 2-1 at Loftus Road, with both goals coming from Rumarn Burrell.

QPR arrived in fourth place on 7 points, having won away at Portsmouth 3-1 and Blackburn Rovers 2-1 and drawn 0-0 at home to Bolton Wanderers, after a first round Carabao Cup exit against Millwall. Cardiff City sat 18th on 3 points, with a first round cup win over Swindon Town 3-2 offset by a second round defeat at home to Norwich City and three league draws, against Wrexham, Sheffield United and away at Derby County.

Julien Stephan made five changes, bringing in Tariq Lampty, Koki Saito, Glen Kamara, Harvey Vale and Alfie Lloyd for Jake Clarke-Salter, Ilias Chair, Paul Smyth, Dennis Cirkin and Richard Kone. Brian Barry-Murphy made one, Calum Scanlon replacing Krystian Bielik. Kamara made his home debut in the QPR midfield.

It was a fast and open start. Ronnie Edwards, deployed in the back four alongside Jimmy Dunne, saw a left-footed effort deflected behind and Dunne then headed narrowly wide from the resulting corner. Yousef Salech drew a good save from Pierce Charles, the goalkeeper on a season-long loan from Manchester City, and Alfie Lloyd fired over at the other end. Cardiff pressed through Alex Robertson, Rubin Colwill and Cian Ashford without reward.

QPR broke the deadlock in the 36th minute. A short corner from Nicolas Madsen was worked to the near post and Burrell forced the ball home from close range after Cardiff failed to clear inside their own six-yard box. The hosts led 1-0 at the break, Harvey Vale twice shooting wide either side of the goal.

Cardiff introduced Chris Willock at half time and Ollie Tanner was booked in the 47th minute for a foul on Lampty. In the 53rd minute QPR doubled the lead, Edwards releasing Burrell for a crisp right-footed finish from inside the area and his second of the afternoon. Burrell went close to a hat-trick with a dribble and shot wide, and Lampty drew a save from Nathan Trott as Rangers chased a third.

Stephan made a triple change on 71 and 72 minutes, sending on Jonathan Varane, Clarke-Salter, Kealey Adamson and Cirkin. In the 74th minute Cardiff pulled one back, Perry Ng steering in a left-footed finish from Tanner's assist to make it 2-1. Ng was then booked for handball in the 77th minute.

Cardiff finished the stronger. Joel Colwill shot wide with his first touch after coming on, and Barry-Murphy threw on Callum Robinson and Isaak Davies in the 88th minute. Chair fired wide from eight yards in added time and QPR looked nervous as the visitors piled forward, but the hosts held on for the three points.

QPR host Middlesbrough next, while Cardiff City travel to Portsmouth.
